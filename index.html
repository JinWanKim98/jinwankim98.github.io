Adjusted comparisons

Singapore HDB resale analysis

Python · pandas · matplotlib · Power BI

I analysed 216,375 Singapore resale transactions, checking how comparisons change when location and year are taken into account. For 4-room flats, the storey–price slope falls from about SGD 132.8 to SGD 87 per sqm per floor after adjustment for town and year. This is an association, not a causal premium.

Query plans

PostgreSQL tuning, PL/pgSQL and MongoDB

PostgreSQL · PL/pgSQL · MongoDB

I designed indexes for ten course-supplied queries and compared their execution plans before and after. Two retained sequential scans despite the new indexes, reporting the same 7,836 shared buffer accesses on both runs: the index existed, but the planner did not choose it here. The repository also includes PL/pgSQL and MongoDB exercises.

Precision–recall trade-off

Predicting late shipments

Python · scikit-learn · Keras

On a 2,200-order test set, Gaussian Naive Bayes flags 582 orders with 95.5% precision; the neural network flags 986 with 78.3% precision. I compared both with a baseline that flags every order. The results show why accuracy alone cannot choose a follow-up strategy; capacity and costs are not supplied.
