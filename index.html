Funnel & A/B test

Customer conversion and email experiment

Python · SQL · statsmodels · Power BI

Using event logs from an electronics store, I measured how often shoppers went from view to cart to purchase. Overall, 3.67% of session-product groups completed all three steps in order. For TVs it was 1.34%, a gap worth investigating rather than proof of a page problem. I kept the 41.67% of events with no brand or category under an “Unknown” label instead of dropping them. In a separate randomized email experiment, the Mens-merchandise email raised the purchase rate by 0.6805 percentage points compared with no email, and the Womens-merchandise email by 0.3111 points.

Financial statements

Visa vs Mastercard, FY2021–2025

Python · SQL · Excel · Power BI

Built from public SEC filings and annual reports. Visa’s FY2025 revenue grew 11.34%, but its operating margin fell 5.69 percentage points to 59.99%. More than half of the cost increase came from a larger litigation provision. In my Excel model, 4% revenue growth with a 56% operating margin would give Visa USD 23,296m in operating income, below the actual USD 23,994m. These are my assumptions, not forecasts.

Rating quality & review sampling

Content safety rating quality

Python · SQL · Power BI

Google’s DICES-350 dataset has 350 chatbot conversations, each rated by 123 people. Raters matched the expert safety label in 56.92% of 43,050 judgments. With a budget of 35 conversations for a second review, random selection found on average 10.02% of the judgments that missed unsafe content, while a fixed most-severe-first rule found only 2.90%.

  • Precision–recall trade-off

    Predicting late shipments

    Python · scikit-learn · Keras

    On a 2,200-order test set, Gaussian Naive Bayes flags 582 orders with 95.5% precision; the neural network flags 986 with 78.3% precision. I compared both with a baseline that flags every order. The results show why accuracy alone cannot choose a follow-up strategy: which model fits depends on how many alerts a team can follow up on.
