Adjusted comparisons

Singapore HDB resale analysis

Python · pandas · matplotlib · Power BI

216,375 resale transactions from January 2017 to September 2025. How strongly storey height tracks price depends on what you hold constant: comparing 4-room flats within the same town and year gives a slope of about SGD 87 per sqm per floor, against about SGD 132.8 before that adjustment, so the raw slope is roughly 53% larger. Ten floors at the adjusted slope come to 15.7% of the sample mean price per sqm, which is an association across the sample rather than a return on any one flat.

Precision–recall trade-off

Predicting late shipments

Python · scikit-learn · Keras

10,999 e-commerce orders, where the target value 1 means late. Flagging every order gives 100% recall at 59.68% accuracy across 2,200 orders, which is why recall on its own says little; Gaussian Naive Bayes flags 582 orders at 0.955 precision, and the small neural network flags 986 at 0.783. Which operating point is right depends on follow-up capacity and the cost of a missed delay, and the dataset supplies neither.

Feature engineering

Rain in Australia

Python · pandas · scikit-learn

A four-person group project on 145,460 daily Australian weather observations; my part was the exploration, cleaning, derived features and the preprocessing pipeline, while other members did the modelling. Plotting missing rate against target correlation showed that missingness alone is not a reason to discard a column: the sunshine and cloud readings are among the most incomplete and still show a useful association with next-day rain, while Evaporation is similarly incomplete and sits low on that association; I dropped it as an exploratory choice. I put the features I derived behind a WeatherFeatureAdder(add_features=True/False) switch so the model search decided whether to keep them, and across three model families the controlled diagnostic came out mixed, so it does not establish a consistent improvement.

Baseline comparison

Wine quality regression

Python · scikit-learn

A five-person group project; I implemented the linear regression baseline — its pipeline, its small parameter search and its performance report — while other members built the random forest, gradient boosting and the classification side. Exact duplicate rows were removed first, leaving 1,359 red and 3,961 white observations before an 80/20 split. On white wine the random forest cut test RMSE by about 6.6% against OLS while carrying a much larger training gap; on red, the 0.0010 RMSE between the two ensembles is too small to rank them from a single split.
